Privacy policy

Dig Site: Fossil Museum · last updated 18 November 2026

Dig Site is made to be played without handing over anything about you. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts, and the game doesn't collect any data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your finds, your museum and your settings are saved on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Looks are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only learns which looks you own.

Sharing

Postcards are shared only when you tap Share, through the iPhone's own share sheet, to wherever you choose. If you choose Save Image, the game asks for permission to add the picture to Photos; it can't see your photos.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: kennyjallison@gmail.com.
